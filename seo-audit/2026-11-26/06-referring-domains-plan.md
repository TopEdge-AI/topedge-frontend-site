## 2. Is the spam dangerous, and should we disavow?

- Search Console Manual actions: **No issues detected** (read 26 Nov 2026). Security issues: none.
- Google's position: its systems ignore spammy links, and disavow is for links you built or paid for that have caused, or are likely to cause, a manual action.
- **Recommendation: do not disavow now** (same as 24 Nov). Triggers to reconsider: a manual action for unnatural links; anyone confirming they bought links for topedgeai.com (if an agency or freelancer sold an "SEO package" between April and September 2026, tell me, because then those domains should be disavowed); or rankings dropping on a date that matches a jump in spam domains.
- **Owner question:** did TopEdge AI ever pay for a backlink, DR or guest-post package (April to September 2026 is when these domains first appear)? A yes changes the recommendation.

## 3. What not to do (each one is a reason sites drop to zero)

- No bought links, "DR boost", guest-post packages, PBNs, link exchanges or paid placements. Huratips, for example, has an "Advertise" page; never pay for a list position.
- No mass directory blasting. A few real SaaS directories are fine; hundreds of junk ones are link spam.
- No guest posts on topedgeai.com by others (site reputation abuse), and no seeded Reddit or forum posts.
- Never ask for "dofollow"; never guess an email address; at most 20 sends a week including follow-ups; one follow-up after 7 to 10 days.
