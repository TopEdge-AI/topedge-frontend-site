# Detailed plan, Days 6 to 14 (written 10 Nov 2026)

Volumes are India, OpenSEO, read 10 Nov 2026. Positions are Search Console, 30 days to 8 Nov. Anything marked Estimate is my arithmetic, not a measurement. No ranking is promised.

## 1. Who we want and how they actually search

The buyer is a founder or growth lead at an Indian Shopify D2C brand, 100 to 1,500 orders a month, heavy COD, real RTO cost. They reach a tool like ours through four doors. Only one of them is a normal Google search.

| Door | What they do | What decides it | Our state |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Shopify App Store | Search inside the store: "whatsapp", "cod", "abandoned cart" | Reviews and listing text | 0 reviews showing. Biggest gap |
| 2. AI assistants | Long prompts: "which app confirms COD orders on Shopify India" | App Store listing, third-party lists, clear dated facts on our pages | Cited as a source, rarely recommended |
| 3. Google, evaluating | "wati pricing", "interakt alternative", "zoko vs", "whatsapp api pricing" | A dated, honest page that answers in two lines | 2 pricing pages built, 10 alternative posts, most on page 2 |
| 4. Google, solving a problem | "rto in ecommerce", "partial cod", "ecommerce profit calculator", "whatsapp link generator" | A page or tool that does the job | 1 tool indexed, RTO page missing |

What this means: the money searches ("shopify cod app", "cod verification shopify", "shopify whatsapp app") are 10 to 30 a month each. Nobody wins traffic there. We keep those pages sharp for doors 1 and 2, and we earn Google traffic from doors 3 and 4, where the volume is.

## 3. The on-page standard every touched page must meet

1. One primary keyword from the map. It appears in the H1 or the first sentence, and in the meta description. Never in a second page's title.
2. First two or three sentences answer the question in the title. A number and a date where the page is about price.
3. One thing only TopEdge AI can say: a dated reading, a worked rupee bill at 100, 500 and 1,500 orders, or a real screenshot.
4. One table a reader (or an AI) can lift whole.
5. "Where the other tool is better" or "Honest limits", in plain words.
6. Links: up to its hub in the first 200 words, down or sideways to two related pages, one link to /pricing.
7. FAQ only from real queries (Search Console, People also ask). Two or three sentences each.
8. Short paragraphs, no em dashes, brand written "TopEdge AI".
9. Titles: leave alone on comparison pages and on the best-apps post. Elsewhere 65 characters or fewer, description 110 to 160.
10. After build: check in preview, then IndexNow after deploy, then give you the URL for Request indexing.

## 4. Off-page programme, in order of value

1. **App Store reviews.** Ask real merchants, no incentive. Three to five reviews make the rating show, and that listing is what AI assistants read. This outranks every email below.
2. **Finish what is half done.** Digismoothie now lists us (ask for the topedgeai.com link). SaaSHub and G2 need your sign-in. App Store docs link still points at the old docs host.
3. **List inclusion, 5 emails at a time.** Drafts for ControlF5, Sumtracker, Logbase and Sociolabs are ready. Next batch (Day 10): Marketian, EasyInsights, Base, Releasit, EasySell. Each page is re-read on the day and the first line proves it.
4. **Give people something to cite.** The link generator and the RTO calculator are the two assets other sites link to without being asked. The RTO benchmark post is the third.
5. **YouTube demos.** Four screen recordings, 2 to 3 minutes each (COD confirmation, cart recovery, COD to prepaid, P&L). This is the real answer to the "video isn't on a watch page" report, and assistants cite YouTube.
6. **Bing Webmaster Tools.** Import from Search Console. Copilot and ChatGPT search lean on Bing's index.
7. **Not doing:** disavow, paid links, new link exchanges, seeded Reddit posts, directory blasts.
