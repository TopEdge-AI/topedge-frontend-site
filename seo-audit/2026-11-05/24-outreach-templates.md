# Outreach templates and rules, TopEdge AI

Written 2026-11-05. Nothing here has been sent, submitted or posted. Prospects are in `23-outreach-prospects.csv`.

Use this sentence word for word wherever a description is asked for:

> TopEdge AI is a Shopify app for Indian D2C brands that automates abandoned cart recovery, COD confirmation and order updates over WhatsApp and email.

## Rules before any send

1. **One follow-up only.** Send it 7 to 10 days later, two lines, then stop. No reply is an answer.
2. **Maximum 20 sends a week, follow-ups included.** The CSV schedules 20 new pitches in days 1 to 7 and 20 in days 8 to 14. Follow-ups from week 1 land in week 2, so unsent week 2 rows slide right. Rows marked BACKLOG or HELD are not in those 40.
3. **Personalise the first line** with something only someone who read the page would know. If you cannot, do not send.
4. **Check their page again on the day.** It was read on 2026-11-05 and these pages change.
5. **Never ask for dofollow, and never mention link attributes.** Ask to be considered. Do not offer anything in return.

## What not to do

- **No link exchanges at scale.** Google's spam policies name them. The Digismoothie and Rahul deals are the limit, and neither should be repeated as a pattern.
- **No paid dofollow links.** No guest-post fees, link insertions, "editorial review" packages or DR boosts. If a reply quotes a price, reply no thanks and mark the row paid. A paid placement must carry `rel="sponsored"`, and the owner is not buying those anyway.
- **No incentivised reviews.** No discount, credit or gift for an App Store, G2, Capterra or Trustpilot review. It violates Shopify's review policy and puts the listing at risk.
- **No press-release syndication, no astroturfing.** In forums always say you build TopEdge.
- **No invented numbers.** Anything marked [NEEDS REAL DATA] stays unsent until a real, approved figure replaces it. Do not use percentages from competitor blogs as if they were yours.
- **No guessed email addresses.** Use only the route in the CSV.

## Limits

- **Not opened:** Capterra and GetApp vendor pages (bot challenge everywhere; the row points to the G2 portal), and Reddit r/shopify, which the browser pane refuses and curl cannot read. Reddit is left out of the CSV for that reason.
- **Rank column is sparse.** Figures come from the CSVs and audit files (OpenSEO's rank, or DR where the audit said so). Those scales differ. The Ahrefs free DR call returned "Insufficient plan", so nothing was estimated.
- **Rel coverage varies.** Listicles were read from raw HTML. "dofollow generally" on media rows means sitewide outbound links, not what they would give a contributor. Partner and agency rows are often "not read".
- **Link type matters.** Many lists link only to apps.shopify.com. That helps the AI-cited listing but adds no referring domain to topedgeai.com, and each row's rel_seen says which.
- **Web search hit its limit** near the end, so discovery of more newsletters stopped. Only two active Indian D2C newsletters are confirmed, and more may exist.
- **Reader numbers are unverified.** No traffic data was available, so "real article with readers" is judged from content, dates and authorship.
- **Three podcast emails** come from public RSS feeds, not the show's site, and each row says so.
