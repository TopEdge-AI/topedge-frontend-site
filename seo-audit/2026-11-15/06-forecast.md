# Forecast: what 14, 30 and 90 days can deliver (15 Nov 2026)

**Labels.** Inputs are Verified (Search Console as given, OpenSEO India volumes read 10 Nov, SERPs read 15 Nov). Every forecast number is an **Estimate**. No ranking is promised.

**Starting point (Verified):** about 25 impressions a day and about 9 clicks a month on the commercial clusters. Best query "best whatsapp app for shopify" 57 impressions at 17.5. Last 28 days: India 224 impressions, US 126.

**Why 10,000 to 20,000 impressions a day is not reachable in a month.** Every keyword in `03-content-gaps.csv` with a known India volume adds up to about **37,000 searches a month, about 1,240 a day** (2,340 a day if "whatsapp link", 33,100, is counted, and that one is mostly people looking for wa.me, not buyers). A page on page 1 earns roughly one impression per search. So even ranking top 10 for *every* gap at once would give about 1,200 to 2,300 India impressions a day. 10k to 20k a day needs 5 to 10 times the whole keyword universe of this business, which means consumer terms ("whatsapp web", "bulk whatsapp sender") that bring no Shopify buyers. (Estimate, arithmetic on Verified volumes.)

**Clicks (Estimate):** 14 days 0.5 to 1.5 a day; 30 days 2 to 5 a day; 90 days 10 to 25 a day. AI Overviews on pricing and "what is" queries will take a share of these.

**The one-line answer for the owner:** page 1 in 14 days is realistic for 3 to 5 low-volume, India-modified queries, not for any query with real volume. Plan around 60 to 150 impressions a day in a month and a few hundred a day by February.
