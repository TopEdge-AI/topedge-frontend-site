## 1. The principle

You have no authority to spend, so you cannot win links by asking. You win
them by being (a) listed where listings are free and expected, (b) cited
because you published a number nobody else has, and (c) included in lists that
already rank.

In that order, because that is also cheapest-first.

---

## 2. Tier 1: free, legitimate, do in the next two weeks

These are directory and profile links. They are not glamorous and they are not
optional: they are also how Google resolves you as an **entity**, which is the
other thing that is broken.

| Target | Why it matters here | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| **Shopify App Store listing** | The ecosystem surface for your actual category. Optimise title, subtitle, keywords, screenshots, video, FAQ. | 2h |
| **G2** | Claim it. The existing profile describes you as "a technology company specializing in AI solutions", which is both wrong and the BSP framing you are trying to escape. | 1h |

**Rule for all of them:** identical brand name, identical one-sentence
description, identical category. Inconsistency here is why the brand entity is
unresolved. Use exactly:

> TopEdge AI is a Shopify app for Indian D2C brands that automates abandoned
> cart recovery, COD confirmation and order updates over WhatsApp and email.

Note what that sentence does: **Shopify app** is the noun, WhatsApp is a
channel at the end. Use it verbatim everywhere, including the App Store
subtitle and the G2 description.

## 3. Tier 2: the shopify.com link

DelightChat has 56 backlinks from shopify.com, first seen 2023. You have zero.
This is the most valuable and most legitimate link in the category.

They come from Shopify's own surfaces: the App Store listing itself, app
collections, the Shopify blog, partner and developer pages, community answers.
You cannot buy any of it. You get it by being a well-reviewed app that Shopify
editorial has reason to reference.

Which puts **App Store reviews** on the critical path for links, not just for
conversion. You are at **0**. Five merchants already reviewed you on Trustpilot
(4.0 from 5) and three are named on the site with live stores. That is an ask,
not a project, and it has been outstanding since 7 October.

Do that before anything in Tier 3.

## 4. Tier 3: the one asset that earns links by itself

Everything above is table stakes that competitors also have. This is the part
that creates a reason to link.

**Publish an India D2C COD and RTO benchmark from your own aggregated data.**

You are sitting on something nobody else in this market has: real cart,
COD-confirmation and RTO outcomes across Indian Shopify stores. Nobody can
write that post but you. Journalists, competitors and AI answer engines all
cite original numbers, and they cite nothing else reliably.

Conditions, and they are not negotiable:
- Aggregated and anonymised, with merchant terms and privacy checked first.
- Minimum store count stated, and a published methodology.
- Real numbers only. If the sample is too small to be honest, say so and wait.
- Date it, and commit to refreshing it annually.

Two existing assets support this and are already indexed:
`/tools/rto-cost-calculator` and `/tools/cart-recovery-calculator`. A benchmark
post gives both a reason to be linked.

If the data is not there yet, the fallback is a survey of 100+ Indian D2C
operators. Slower, weaker, still original.

## 5. Tier 4: inclusion in lists that already rank

From the SERPs pulled for this audit, these pages rank on page 1 for queries
you want and are maintained by people who accept corrections:

- `delightchat.io/blog/zoko-alternatives` (ranks #1 for "zoko alternative")
- `fixmystore.com/shopify-apps/alternatives/...` (ranks #1 for "getkanal alternatives")
- `theindustryoutlook.com` "10 Best RTO Prevention Tools for Shopify Stores in India"
- `hillteck.com` "How to Reduce RTO for Indian D2C Brands"
- `softpulseinfotech.com` "4 Best Order Confirmation Apps for Shopify"
- `magecomp.com` "7 Best WhatsApp Apps for Shopify"
- plus the "Best WhatsApp API Providers in India (2026)" cluster

**How to pitch, and this matters more than the list.** Do not ask for a link.
Send a specific, checkable correction or addition: your plan prices with the
date you read theirs, the COD-to-prepaid capability most of these lists do not
cover, and one sentence on who you are *not* for. Editors publish corrections
far more readily than they publish favours.

Realistic conversion: 2 to 4 inclusions out of 12 pitches. That is a good
outcome, not a poor one.

## 6. Tier 5: slower, still real

- **Integration and partner pages.** Every real integration (Shiprocket,
  payment providers, helpdesks) usually has a partner directory. Publish a
  matching integration page on your side. Only list integrations that exist.
- **Indian startup and ecommerce press.** Inc42, YourStory, Entrackr. Pitch the
  §4 benchmark or a founder point of view, never "please cover us".
- **Reddit r/shopify**, which holds position 8 for "whatsapp cart recovery
  shopify". Answer real questions, disclose that you build TopEdge, link only
  when it genuinely answers the question. Never astroturf: Google filters
  inauthentic mentions and it would damage the entity you are trying to build.
- **Unlinked mentions.** Weak for you right now, because there are few
  mentions to convert. Revisit after §4 lands.

## 7. What not to do

No purchased links, PBNs, link farms, large-scale exchanges, comment spam,
expired domains or "DR boost" packages. You already have 37 spam domains
pointing at you without having bought any; adding more is how a thin profile
becomes a penalised one. Any sponsored placement must carry `rel="sponsored"`
and be disclosed.

Do not disavow wanderlog, magicpin, bestsalons, khojosale or shoplist (§0).
