# AI visibility baseline, 2026-11-02 (complete)

Run `968931ea-8936-4b17-9766-6934748720b3`. **100 of 100 observations, 0
failed.** 20 prompts x 5 engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Claude,
Perplexity), India, $6.02.

Numbers below are the 80 **neutral** (non-brand) answers. Brand prompts are
excluded because "what is TopEdge AI" naming TopEdge proves nothing.

| Brand | Mentioned in | Cited as source |

| **TopEdge AI** | **7 / 80** | **12** |
| Updatrr | 5 / 80 | 6 |

Read those two columns together, because the gap between them is the whole
finding.

**TopEdge is cited as a source more than any competitor (12), and recommended
less than almost all of them (7).** Interakt gets named five times more often
while being cited half as much.

So the content is good enough that engines read it and quote facts from it.
It is not what they reach for when a buyer asks "which app should I use". That
is not a content-quality problem, and writing more pages will not fix it.

## 4. Bitespeed: there is nothing to copy

The owner asked which keywords Bitespeed ranks for so we can target them.
Verified answer: **Bitespeed ranks for four keywords in India, and not one is
commercial.**

| Keyword | Volume | Position | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| bitespeed photos | 880 | 3 | their own brand |
| bitespeed bangalore | 590 | 2 | their office address |
| bitespeed login | 390 | 1 | their login page |
| betyphon | 320 | 49 | a different company's brand |

Total India organic traffic: **359 visits a month**. They do not rank for
"bitespeed alternative", for cart recovery, or for anything a buyer searches
before choosing an app.

**There is no Bitespeed keyword strategy to copy, because they do not have
one.** This matches the earlier finding that qualified Shopify-app demand in
India is small and buyers do not arrive through Google.

Worth noting what *does* rank for "bitespeed alternative": com.bot and
techmonk.io, both third-party listicles. Same pattern as every other
comparison query. Inclusion in those lists is the play, not outranking them.

## 5. What this changes

1. **App Store reviews move to the top of everything.** §2 proves the
   mechanism: 31 of 80 AI answers read apps.shopify.com, and our listing is
   empty. Five Trustpilot reviewers and three named merchants are already
   willing.
2. **Stop treating "more content" as the AI-visibility lever.** We are already
   the 3rd most-cited domain. Citations are not the constraint.
3. **Contest the India-first slot in §3.** Those AI Overviews are answering a
   question we answer better. The route is third-party corroboration, not more
   of our own pages.
4. **Drop the idea of copying Bitespeed's keywords** (§4).
