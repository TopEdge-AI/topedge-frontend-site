1. Executive summary

  • Verified: India, 25 Oct to 21 Nov: "best whatsapp app for shopify" 41 impressions, 7 clicks, CTR 17.1%, average position 21.5. The page earns clicks when seen; it is seen too rarely because it sits on page 3.
  • Verified: on-page tool score 85 against 39 (slashdot.org), 67 (ampmails.com) and 69 (go-apps.io). On-page setup is not the limiter. The fixable misses: singular keyword not in description, first 100 words or any H2. Title and H1 are locked by the owner's rule.
  • Verified, the biggest finding: the page was wrong in five places. Getgabs, ranked #2 and linked four times, was "not currently available on the Shopify App Store". Dondy's free plan is a chat button, not a messaging trial. DelightChat now lists cart recovery, COD verification and COD to prepaid on every plan. Zoko changed its markup model. Updatrr's "no markup" line is gone. A list that names the wrong #2 is the thing a reader and an AI answer engine would both distrust.
  • Verified gap: the #1 India result for "whatsapp app for shopify" is Meta's own free WhatsApp app (apps.shopify.com/whatsapp, 4.4 from 986). The page never mentioned it.
  • Hypothesis: position will move only with links and reviews as well; on-page now answers the query better than the three pages above it.
