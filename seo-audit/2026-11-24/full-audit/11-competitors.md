# Competitor Analysis Report: TopEdge AI
**Prepared by SearchFit.ai** | Fetched 24 Nov 2026 | Read-only; no site or repo changes made

## Executive summary

Eleven sites were fetched today, plus six TopEdge AI pages for comparison. TopEdge AI's largest gap is proof, not page count. Its customers page has two case studies with no numbers, while Zoko shows 13 metric-led cards, QuickReply 40+, LimeChat 42 and AiSensy 56. Rivals lead with Shopify App Store ratings (Zoko 4.9 from 450, Updatrr 4.9, DelightChat 4.9 from 200+). TopEdge AI leads with Trustpilot 4.0 from 5 reviews.

The second gap is page anatomy. The TopEdge AI COD feature page has one poster image and no product UI or customer quote. QuickReply's RTO page runs problem, four campaigns, how it works, case cards, reviews, trust badges and FAQ.

The third gap is supporting content: integration detail pages (Updatrr has 10, TopEdge AI has 1), a changelog (DelightChat and Kaching publish several entries a week), a glossary, and template pages with copy buttons.

TopEdge AI already beats most rivals on dated, sourced claims and INR pricing transparency. Bitespeed, LimeChat and QuickReply hide or fail to render prices, and Updatrr's pages carry visible errors.

Two limits: CSS was not readable through the fetch tool, so the design section has no measurements. Bitespeed has no sitemap.

## Method and limits

- **Labels:** Verified = fetched 24 Nov 2026. Estimate = partial data. Hypothesis = inference.
- **Title tags:** The fetch tool returns page text, not HTML source. Titles marked "(inferred)" come from the first line of page text, and H1 levels are sometimes inferred from position.
- **Sitemap counts:** Large sitemaps were cut off at 100,000 characters. Where that happened I used `seo-audit/COMPETITOR_MATRIX.csv` (captured 25 Oct 2026) and say so.
- **Design measurements:** No stylesheet, font or inline style values came through for kachingappz.com or instantly.ai. Nothing in the design section is a measurement.
- **Guessed URLs that returned 404:** `delightchat.io/alternative/interakt-alternative`, `quickreply.ai/compare/interakt-alternative`, `zoko.io/case-study/velvet-box`. These were my guesses, not broken site links.
- **Not run:** No claude-seo skill phases, Core Web Vitals, schema or E-E-A-T checks. This was research only.

## Design references: kachingappz.com and instantly.ai

No CSS was readable, so there are no font sizes, radii or paddings to report. What follows is verified structure. A 10-minute DevTools pass on both sites would supply the numbers.

### Kaching (Verified structure)
- **Product UI:** Shown as coded HTML mockups, not screenshots. The hero has a working bundle widget with tier cards, "Most popular" labels, savings lines and an "Add to cart" total that changes. A tab row above it switches between six apps.
- **Feature sections:** Each benefit gets its own mockup card showing a state (box builder with progress counter, swap panel modal).
- **Stats row:** Three numbers directly under the hero: 120,000+ stores, $3.3B+ added revenue, 24/7 human support. The same row repeats on the case studies and app pages.
- **Social proof:** One aggregate block (4.9 stars, 10,380 reviews), then a grid of cards with app icon, stars, quote, reviewer name and tenure. One larger testimonial has a before/after price callout and a portrait.
- **Case study cards:** Whole tile is a link. Image, app tag, long headline, short intro, then two metric pairs with a small label above a large value ("Subscription revenue / S$258,400").
- **Support as a section:** "Real people. Ready to help." with a support-desk mockup showing agents online and open tickets.
- **Freshness blocks:** "What's new" changelog cards with dates on home and app pages. The changelog has 27 pages and near-daily entries.
- **Learning block:** One featured video plus a numbered playlist of five with durations.
- **App page extras:** 3-step "first offer to first sale", a "we'll help you switch" block, a pricing slider with a "Show all 7 plans" toggle, 7 FAQs, closing CTA with "7 days free · No code needed".
- **Buttons:** Two actions repeated throughout ("Explore apps", "Talk to our team"). Section links are text-style ("See more stories").
- **Footer:** Five short columns (Apps, Explore, Company, Resources, Legal), one rating line, two social icons.
- **Integrations page:** Filter tabs, 24 cards with logo, name, one line and a "works with" row; 19 have detail pages.

### Instantly (Verified structure)
- **Build:** Probably Webflow; assets are served from `cdn.prod.website-files.com`.
- **Hero:** Two-word H1 ("Find Clients"), one-line subhead, a prompt input with three suggestion chips, one button.
- **Logo strip:** About 16 grey logos under one line of text.

### What makes both look premium (Hypothesis, from structure)
- One primary action label repeated on every section.
- Very short headings, with detail pushed into the visual.
- Product shown in a realistic state with real numbers, not an illustration.
- Proof placed immediately under the hero and again before the close.
- Long detail (pricing tables, plan lists) collapsed behind a toggle.

These fit TopEdge AI's existing rules: short heroes, detail in FAQ accordions, purple pill CTAs.
