**This file is the one plan and the one log.** From 24 Nov 2026 the plan runs to Day 37: section 8 holds the 30-day plan, the working method and the care list, written from the full audit in `2026-11-24/full-audit/`. It lives in the repo so every Claude account and session can read it. Every session reads it first and writes its day's log row at the end. ## 1. Goal

Move our ICP pages that already rank on Google pages 2 to 5 (average position 11 to 50) up to page 1. No new pages for impressions' sake.

- **ICP only:** Indian Shopify D2C brands, 100 to 1,500 orders a month, heavy COD. Queries about WhatsApp for Shopify, cart recovery, COD, RTO, and named rivals (Interakt, WATI, AiSensy, Bitespeed, Zoko, Updatrr).
- **Ignore:** spa and salon queries (other businesses' listings), /blog/shopify-seo-experts and /blog/best-shopify-upsell-apps traffic, "getkanal alternatives" (non-India noise), consumer queries (whatsapp web, qr code download), Meta Flows ("whatsapp flows shopify"), and the traps in DAILY-PROMPT section 4.
- **No ranking is promised.** Pages that reach page 1 usually need links and reviews as well as on-page work.

**Revised 20 Nov 2026 (owner):** two tracks run every day from Day 6.

- **Track A, one blog page a day:** the full audit (steps 5 and 6 below) on one ICP blog post, picked by ICP value first, then Search Console position.
- **Track B, one keyword landing page a day (Days 6 to 10), then review:** build or upgrade one page from the landing page map in section 3b. Each page targets one commercial ICP keyword with the exact keyword in the URL, title, H1 and the line under the H1, and is good enough to send Meta and Google ads to.
- **Keyword priority:** product keywords a buyer types when choosing a tool ("whatsapp abandoned cart recovery app", "shopify cod app", "whatsapp app for shopify", "shopify whatsapp integration", "whatsapp order notification shopify", "rto reduction") come before rival-versus-rival comparisons. "aisensy vs gallabox" and "wati vs aisensy" are not our keywords: no new work on them beyond accuracy fixes.
- **Home page and feature pages are frozen** except for factual corrections, and a title or H1 fix when a feature page is the owner of a landing-page keyword (only /features/cod-confirmation today). Ranking work happens on blog posts and landing pages, the way most SaaS sites do it.
- **Google policies:** every page passes the people-first gate and the spam-policy table in `2026-11-20/01-google-ai-content-guidance.md` section 7. Doorway abuse is the specific risk for landing pages: each one must answer its own query fully and differ in substance.

4. **Ahrefs site audit** (built-in browser, app.ahrefs.com, project 10574646): health score, errors and warnings. Fix real errors on topedgeai.com; ignore dash.topedgeai.com rows. If it shows the login page, tell the owner and move on.

6. **Day's page, the full audit:**
   1. Save its Search Console export: `seo-audit/<date>/page-audits/<page>/gsc-export-*.csv` (query, clicks, impressions, CTR, position; India 28 days and all countries 3 months).
   2. Run the on-page audit tool against the top 3 India results that rank above us:
      `node seo-audit/tools/onpage-audit.mjs <our-url> "<keyword>" seo-audit/<date>/page-audits/<page>/before <comp1> <comp2> <comp3>`

   3. Run `seo-audit/PAGE-ANALYSIS-PROMPT.md` on that export and the tool output. Write the report as HTML and render it: `node seo-audit/tools/html-to-pdf.mjs <in.html> <out.pdf>` → `analysis-report.pdf`.

   3b. **Question research and question-first structure** (owner, 24 Nov 2026): list the real questions for the page from Search Console, People Also Ask and one `get_keyword_metrics` call, with their source. The first H2 above the list or body is the main searched question with a one-line answer; "Which X is best for your Y?" H3s answer long-tail queries; the FAQ is "Frequently asked questions about <topic>" with numbered questions that open with Yes, No or the answer (DAILY-PROMPT 5.16). Applies to every Track A page and every landing page from Day 7.
   4a. People-first gate: `node seo-audit/tools/people-first-audit.mjs dist <day-folder>/people-first --only <route>` on the built page before and after the change; it must not gain a flag (rules in `2026-11-20/01-google-ai-content-guidance.md`).

### 8.5 How every session works (read this before touching anything)

1. Read this file, `DAILY-PROMPT.md`, memory, then `git fetch`, `git status`, `git log`.
2. **Baseline before any change:** `claude-seo run drift_baseline.py --skip-cwv <live-url>` for each page the day will touch. Compare after the deploy with `drift_compare.py`. Title, description, canonical, H1 and schema must be the same unless the day's plan says they change.
3. **Use the skills, not memory.** `claude-seo` for planning, building and verifying (the phases in the repo `CLAUDE.md`); `topedge-positioning` for any sentence that names the product; `google-search-policies` before publishing; `topedge-landing-page`, `topedge-compare-board`, `topedge-seo-day` for their page types.
