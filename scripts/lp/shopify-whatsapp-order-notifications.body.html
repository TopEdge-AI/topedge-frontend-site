What are Shopify WhatsApp order notifications?

They are the WhatsApp messages a Shopify store sends on its own when an order is confirmed, shipped, out for delivery and delivered. Each one is a Meta utility template at ₹0.1150 in India (re-read 16 November 2026), so three updates on an order cost about ₹0.35. Shopify’s own notifications go by email and SMS, so WhatsApp needs an app.
