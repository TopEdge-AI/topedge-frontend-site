What is a Shopify WhatsApp integration?

It is the link that lets Shopify store events send WhatsApp messages from your own number: an order placed becomes a confirmation, an abandoned checkout becomes a reminder, a COD order becomes a Confirm or Cancel question. It needs two approvals, one in Shopify admin for store data and one in Meta’s embedded signup for your WhatsApp Business Account.

What each Shopify permission is for in TopEdge AI
Permission groupWhat it enables
Orders (read and write)Order journeys and triggers, store analytics, and tagging, cancelling or marking an order paid from the inbox
Customers and checkoutsThe audience list, matching buyers by phone number, and abandoned checkout detection for cart recovery
Products and inventoryThe product catalogue for flows and templates, and stock levels
FulfilmentsShipped and delivered journeys with tracking numbers
Draft orders and discountsCOD to prepaid payment links, cart recovery invoices and recovery discount codes
Pixels and customer eventsThe storefront web pixel that captures carts and browse events
