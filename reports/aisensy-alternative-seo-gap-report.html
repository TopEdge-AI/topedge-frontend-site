The single sentence version

A page titled “Best AiSensy Alternative” named exactly one alternative, ours, in 1,108 words, while the pages beating it averaged 2,929 words and compared five or six products each. Everything else in this report is downstream of that.

The five findings that matter

  1. Content depth gap of 1,821 words. 1,108 against a competitor average of 2,929, and against 3,765 on AiSensy’s own page, which is the incumbent for its own brand query. CONFIRMED
  2. The keyword was absent from four of the six places Google reads it. Not in the meta description, not in the first 100 words, not in any H2, not in any link anchor. It appeared twice in 1,108 words. CONFIRMED
  3. The meta description was being truncated. 1,003 px against a desktop cut of roughly 920 px, so the end of the snippet never reached a searcher. CONFIRMED
  4. The page did not serve the queries that surround the main one. “aisensy alternative free”, “aisensy alternative reddit” and the comparison names (Interakt, WATI, Gallabox, Twilio) had no section, no heading and in three cases no mention at all. CONFIRMED
  5. Topical coverage was thin against competitor vocabulary. Nine phrases and more than twenty words used repeatedly by two or three competitors never appeared on the page, including chatbot builder, team inbox, official WhatsApp, free plan and qualify leads. CONFIRMED

04  Critical issues, with the fix for each

C-01  Meta description truncated and missing the keyword
P1FIXED

Evidence: 151 characters, 1,003 px measured against a desktop cut of about 920 px, flagged “Too wide. Google will cut it on desktop.” Separately flagged: “Keyword in meta description: Not found.”

Competitor difference: eazybe carries the exact keyword in a 148-character description. whatchimp runs 156 characters without it. You were the only one doing both things wrong at once.

Why it matters: Google bolds query terms in the snippet, which lifts CTR. The old description also spent its visible characters on “Outgrowing AiSensy broadcasts” and put the proof after the cut, so the strongest part never showed.

The fix, as shipped 
// src/data/blogPostsQ4.ts
- 'Outgrowing AiSensy broadcasts on Shopify India? Compare 0% Meta markup
-  (₹0.115 service vs ₹0.145 on AiSensy), BYOK AI, and native COD-to-prepaid flows.'
+ 'AiSensy alternative for Shopify India: AiSensy vs Interakt, WATI,
+  Gallabox and Twilio on price, AI cost and COD to prepaid.'
// 123 chars, 868 px measured in 16px Arial, keyword at position 0

Validate: re-measure the rendered <meta name="description"> in Arial at 16 px; stay under 920 px. Then watch CTR for this URL in Search Console, 28 days before against 28 days after.

C-02  Keyword absent from the first 100 words
P1FIXED

Evidence: “Keyword in first 100 words: Not found.” The opening ran “AiSensy is one of the most visible WhatsApp marketing platforms in India…”, which describes AiSensy rather than the thing the searcher asked for.

Competitor difference: whatchimp places the exact phrase inside the first 100 words. You and the other two did not.

Why it matters: the opening paragraph is weighted heavily for topical relevance, and it is also what an AI Overview or ChatGPT answer lifts as the summary of the page.

The fix, as shipped. The first body sentence now reads: “Most people searching for an AiSensy alternative are not unhappy with AiSensy. They have outgrown the shape of it.” Keyword at word 7, and the sentence states the page’s thesis rather than describing the incumbent.

Validate: extract the first 100 words of rendered body text and assert the phrase is present.

C-03  Keyword in zero H2s, and only 8 H2s against an average of 14
P1FIXED

Evidence: “Keyword in an H2: None.” 8 H2 / 5 H3 against 20/15, 12/17 and 9/31.

Competitor difference: eazybe puts the keyword in eight headings, including “Best WhatChimp Alternative: Eazybe For WhatsApp Sales And CRM”. whatchimp runs sections you had no equivalent for at all: “When AiSensy is the better choice, and when it is not”, “Moving from AiSensy to WhatChimp”, “Your number moves. Your customers notice nothing.”

Why it matters: H2s are how Google maps a page to sub-queries, and how an AI answer picks which chunk to quote. Eight headings cannot cover a six-product comparison.

The fix, as shipped. 19 H2s, six carrying the keyword:
  • What is the best AiSensy alternative for WhatsApp in India?
  • AiSensy alternative comparison: six WhatsApp platforms side by side
  • Is there a free AiSensy alternative?
  • What people actually ask before choosing an AiSensy alternative
  • Features to check before you pick an AiSensy alternative
  • Which AiSensy alternative fits which store

Plus five dedicated head-to-head sections (AiSensy vs Interakt / WATI / Gallabox / Twilio / TopEdge AI) and a migration section answering the number-move objection whatchimp owns.

C-04  993 ms HTML download — measure before you act
P2ASSUMPTION

Evidence: “Page download time: 993 ms to download the full HTML from the audit server.” m.aisensy returned in 67 ms; whatchimp 1,523 ms; eazybe 2,291 ms.

Why this is not yet a confirmed defect: the document is only 68 KB, brotli is on, HSTS is on, and there are zero render-blocking scripts. A 68 KB brotli response taking 993 ms is almost certainly a cold edge node or the audit server’s distance from the CDN, not a server problem. The Netlify config already sets Netlify-CDN-Cache-Control: public, s-maxage=604800, stale-while-revalidate=604800, durable on /*.

What to do, in order
  1. Run the URL through PageSpeed Insights and read the field TTFB, not the lab number. Field data is real users.
  2. curl -o /dev/null -s -w "dns:%{time_namelookup} connect:%{time_connect} ttfb:%{time_starttransfer} total:%{time_total}\n" https://topedgeai.com/blog/aisensy-alternative-shopify-india three times, and read the second and third runs (the first pays for a cold cache).
  3. Check the response for x-nf-request-id and a cache-hit header. A MISS on a repeat request is the real finding.
  4. Only if field TTFB exceeds about 600 ms is there an action, and it is a CDN or hosting change, not a code change.

Effort: 30 minutes to measure. Unknown to fix, and possibly zero.

C-05  Three competitor prices cannot be published, because nobody has read the source
P2CONFIRMED

Evidence: scripts/check-blog-sources.mjs fails the build on any external link whose host is not registered in src/data/sources.ts, which carries the date a human opened the URL. aisensy.com and interakt.shop are registered. wati.io, gallabox.com and twilio.com are not.

Consequence: the WATI, Gallabox and Twilio rows in the new comparison table carry no price and no outbound link. That is the honest position, but it is also a competitive weakness: eazybe and whatchimp both publish competitor plan names and tiers.

The fix
  1. A human opens wati.io/pricing, gallabox.com/pricing and twilio.com/whatsapp/pricing and records what each actually says, with the date.
  2. Add an entry per source to src/data/sources.ts in the existing shape, which puts the host into ALLOWED_EXTERNAL_HOSTS automatically.
  3. Fill the three table cells with the real figures and link each to its source, matching the AiSensy and Interakt rows.
  4. Update the footnote, which currently says those three carry no price on purpose.

Effort: 45 minutes, mostly reading. Impact: high, because a comparison table with three empty price cells is the first thing a sceptical reader notices.

C-06  15 images on a comparison page, against 110 / 46 / 25
P3CONFIRMED

Evidence: 15 images, of which only 5 are content images; the rest are logos, badges and AI-assistant icons. Six of the 15 are still JPG or PNG rather than WebP.

Why it matters: image count is not a ranking factor, but a comparison page with no screenshots of the products being compared reads as written from a distance. It also forfeits Google Images traffic for “aisensy dashboard” style queries.

The fix: add one original screenshot per platform section (six total), each with a descriptive alt containing the platform name, exported as WebP under 100 KB to stay inside scripts/check-image-weight.mjs. Convert the six remaining JPG/PNG assets to WebP at the same time.

08  Step-by-step implementation guidance

For the SEO specialist

Request a recrawl, correctly

  1. Search Console, URL Inspection, paste the full URL of the post.
  2. Confirm the Coverage panel says the URL is on Google and the canonical Google chose matches your declared canonical. A mismatch here outranks everything else in this report.
  3. Test live URL, then View tested page → HTML, and confirm the new H2s and the new meta description are present in the rendered HTML. If they are not, the prerender did not pick up the change and nothing else matters.
  4. Request indexing. Once. Repeated requests do not speed anything up.

Fill the three empty price cells

  1. Open each pricing page and record, with the date: the entry plan name and price, whether messaging is extra, and whether the vendor states a markup on Meta rates.
  2. Add each to src/data/sources.ts. The host is derived automatically into ALLOWED_EXTERNAL_HOSTS, so no second edit is needed.
  3. Fill the cells in the six-platform table and link each figure to its source, exactly as the AiSensy and Interakt rows do.
  4. Amend the footnote sentence that currently explains why those three carry no price.
  5. Run npm run check:blog. It fails on an unregistered host and on em dashes, so this step is self-verifying.

For the developer

Verify the prerendered output, not the SPA

Schema and meta are injected by Helmet and captured by scripts/prerender-marketing.mjs. Any change to post data must be verified in the prerendered file, because that is what a crawler reads:

# build, prerender, then grep the actual artefact
npm run build:static
grep -o '"@type":"BlogPosting"' dist/blog/aisensy-alternative-shopify-india/index.html
grep -o '<meta name="description"[^>]*>'  dist/blog/aisensy-alternative-shopify-india/index.html

Confirm the TTFB story before changing hosting

for i in 1 2 3; do
  curl -o /dev/null -s -w "ttfb:%{time_starttransfer}s total:%{time_total}s\n" \
    https://topedgeai.com/blog/aisensy-alternative-shopify-india
done

Read runs two and three. If TTFB is under about 200 ms warm, the 993 ms figure was the audit server’s distance from the edge and there is no work to do. The existing Netlify-CDN-Cache-Control rule in netlify.toml is already correct and should not be changed speculatively.
